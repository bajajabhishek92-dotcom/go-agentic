# AI Timeline — Theme Index

**Purpose:** Give readers a readable chronology of how the AI space evolved—key eras and inflection points, and what changed for builders and users—without dumping dates or product launch lists.

Grounded primarily in Epoch AI compute/capability/adoption evidence, Stanford HAI AI Index aggregates, Anthropic’s agent-era framing, and supporting IEEE Spectrum / Google Research context.

Each article ends with a **Sources** block of primary URLs (citation pass complete).

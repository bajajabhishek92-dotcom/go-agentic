# Agents & everyday adoption

Companion to the [timeline hub](index.md) / [timeline.html](timeline.html). Covers the current era’s two faces: systems that **act**, and use that became **habit**.

## From chatbots to agents

- Chatbots reply; agents pursue goals across tools, files, browsers, and APIs over many steps.
- Mistakes compound; the artifact that matters is often an **outcome** (passing tests, a booked change), not the final message.
- The **harness**—orchestration, memory, sandboxing, retries, human escalation—is co-equal with model weights.

**Eval shift:** grade trajectories and outcomes; capability evals ≠ regression evals; long-horizon suites (e.g. MirrorCode-style) exist because “pass HumanEval” no longer describes the frontier.

## Adoption

<a id="adoption"></a>

- Near-daily AI use among U.S. adults jumped quickly in Epoch polling; informal free-tier work use often precedes enterprise procurement.
- AI Index: majority of organizations report AI use; investment and regulated deployments (FDA devices, robotaxis) mark “everyday” beyond chat.
- Sentiment and regulation are multi-speed by geography—product localization follows.

**Takeaway:** design for reliability, cost, privacy, and audit logs once users treat AI as default infrastructure.

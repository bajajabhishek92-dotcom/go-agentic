# Compute & capability frontiers

Companion to the [timeline hub](index.md) / [timeline.html](timeline.html). Skim this after the five eras if you want the measurable spine behind product narratives.

## Compute as the spine

- Training run size, chip supply, data-center power, and **inference price** explain *when* eras arrived.
- Epoch: new global AI computing capacity roughly **doubling every ~9 months**; frontier campuses measured in gigawatts.
- Chip geography matters: advanced accelerators concentrate in a few supply chains; customs/import data show *where* capacity landed.

**Why builders care:** access to chips and power constrains who sets the frontier; everyone else adapts, distills, or specializes.

## Plunging price of thought

- A *fixed* capability level can get radically cheaper within months (Epoch “price of thought”; AI Index: GPT-3.5-level inference fell hundreds-fold 2022→2024).
- That price collapse is why assistants scaled to consumer habit and why agents are economically imaginable.

## Capability frontiers

<a id="capability-frontiers"></a>

- Frontiers are **multi-skill**: a model can lead on math and lag on software engineering (Epoch ECI / Astra-style breakdowns).
- **Lead times shrink:** GPT-4 held the longest ECI lead; later reigns are shorter; open-weight gaps often measured in **months**.
- Score jumps need a honesty check: true generalization vs scaffolding vs contamination vs spend-at-inference.

**Takeaway:** track portfolios of hard tasks + price-conditioned access—not one viral leaderboard.

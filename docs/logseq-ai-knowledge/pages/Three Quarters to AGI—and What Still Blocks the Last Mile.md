## Article

- Demis Hassabis’s Sequoia conversation is notable for calibrated optimism: substantial progress toward AGI, with remaining gaps that are conceptual, not only “more scale.”

## What “mostly there” usually means in these talks

- Across Hassabis, Silver, Sutton, and OpenAI/Anthropic builder talks, a shared picture emerges:

- World models and multimodality matter beyond text
- Reasoning and long-horizon agency are recent phase changes
- Continual learning / experience is still underdeveloped relative to static training
- Science and robotics remain harder than coding because data and verifiers are scarcer
- Hassabis’s scientific agenda (and DeepMind peers on scientific AI) treats AGI less as a chatbot end-state and more as a system that can generate and test knowledge in the world.

## Builder takeaway

- Do not plan products as if capabilities are frozen, and do not plan as if every domain is one fine-tune away. Prefer architectures that absorb new model generations, and domains where you can create experience loops and verifiers.

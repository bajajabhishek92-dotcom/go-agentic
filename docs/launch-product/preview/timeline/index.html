AI development timeline

Eras as operating regimes—not product launch laundry lists. Each node opens a teachable.

≤2012

Features & narrow competence

 ~2012–17

Deep learning

Generality as a product; prompting and adaptation partly replace train-from-scratch.

Assistants enter the workflow

Chat as interface; copilots in IDEs and offices; eval pressure spreads beyond academic suites.

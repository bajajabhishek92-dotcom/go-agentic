# Edge AI and Defense Sovereignty Start in the Factory

Stark’s Uwe Horstmann and Johannes Schaback describe European defense modernization as an industrial and software problem: sovereignty is not a press release—it is the ability to **manufacture, update, and operate** intelligent systems under your own control.

## Constraints that teach commercial AI teams

- The buyer (a general) is not the end user (a staff sergeant)—classic dual-customer design
- Edge compute matters when links fail; cloud-only agents are non-starters
- Factories and supply chains are part of the product
- Iteration cycles must respect safety and procurement reality

For AI product builders outside defense, the transferable lesson is ruthless clarity about who the customer is, what “offline” means, and how hardware+software co-evolve.

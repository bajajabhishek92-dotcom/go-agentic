# Voice as a Primary Interface for AI

ElevenLabs’ Mati Staniszewski argues voice is becoming a fundamental interface layer—not a niche accessibility feature. As models speak and listen fluidly, products can meet users in the channel where emotion, speed, and hands-free work already live.

## Why voice now

- Generative speech crossed the uncanny valley for many languages
- Agents need input/output modes beyond keyboards, especially in the physical world
- Media and gaming (Suno, generative video peers) normalize AI-native audio/video creation

## Product constraints

- Latency and interruption handling dominate UX
- Trust and consent for cloned voices are regulatory and brand risks
- Voice without tools is theater; voice + agent harness is a product

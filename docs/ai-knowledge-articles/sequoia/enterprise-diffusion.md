# Enterprise AI Diffusion: Reinvention Beats Feature Packs

Enterprises asked for AI, then rejected much of what they got. Sequoia’s enterprise conversations—Aaron Levie (Box), Elias Torres (Agency), Block’s Goose agent, Crosby in legal—explain why: **diffusion is an org problem**, not a model-demo problem.

## What slows adoption

- Workflow mismatch: copilots that do not change systems of record
- Trust and permissions: agents without clear audit trails
- Unit economics: seats and token burn without outcome pricing
- Change management: managers buy tools; teams protect process

Aaron Levie’s “reinvent yourself in the AI age” framing treats AI as a chance to rebuild product assumptions (content, collaboration, agents over files), not to sprinkle chat widgets. Crosby’s “deal velocity, not billable hours” shows vertical AI winning when it optimizes a business metric incumbents under-serve.

## Practices that work

- Start from a measurable workflow KPI (cycle time, escape rate, recovery time)
- Give agents narrow production scopes with logs humans can correct
- Expect to rebuild internal platforms (IT, identity, eval harnesses)—Serval’s “rebuild IT for AI” theme
- Price for outcomes where possible; token passthrough rarely aligns incentives
